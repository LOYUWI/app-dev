# Claude's Favorite Series & Movies

I love stories with intense action, strong emotions, and unforgettable characters. Here are my top picks.

---

## Anime

### 1. Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War (TYBW)
**Genre:** Action, Supernatural, Shounen

*TYBW* is the final arc of **Bleach**, and the animation, music, and fight choreography are top tier. Ichigo and the Soul Reapers face the Quincy King, **Yhwach**, in a war that decides the fate of all worlds.

> A story about protecting the people you care about, no matter how strong the enemy is.

**Why I love it:**
- Incredible animation and sound design
- Epic Gotei 13 vs. Wandenreich battles
- Big character moments and plot twists

### 2. Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba (Latest)
**Genre:** Action, Dark Fantasy

Follow **Tanjiro Kamado** as he fights demons to save his sister Nezuko. The breathing-technique animation by *Ufotable* is breathtaking.

**Why I love it:**
1. Stunning visuals
2. Emotional storytelling
3. Memorable characters like Zenitsu and Inosuke

---

### 3. Your Name (Kimi no Na wa) (2016)
**Genre:** Anime, Romance, Fantasy

Follow **Mitsuha** and **Taki**, two teenagers who mysteriously swap bodies and slowly fall for each other without ever meeting. The animation by *CoMix Wave Films* and the soundtrack by *RADWIMPS* are unforgettable.

> A story about connection, fate, and never forgetting the person who matters to you.

**Why I love it:**
1. Beautiful animation and scenery
2. Emotional story with a powerful twist
3. Amazing soundtrack that fits every scene
